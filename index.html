Tlaquepaque

Monday, Nov 23
Day trip
⚠️ Barolo 71 — reservation recommended.
  • 10:00amJardín Hidalgo, Andador Turístico, Earth Heart shopping (high-end artisan gallery, opens 10am) and the Andador Independencia strip for glass and pottery.

Day trip

Colonia Americana

Friday, Nov 27
Farewell dinner
⚠️ Santo Coyote — reservation strongly recommended (famous, fills up). Mecenas — reservation recommended for drinks.
  • 9:00amBrunch at Santo Coyote (opens 8am, reserve ahead) — a visually dramatic, immersive space famous citywide for its brunch spread and mariachi; reviewers specifically call out how popular and photogenic it is.
  • 11:00amShopping at Witch mx and Amor Apache — both are well-curated, locally-designed boutiques rather than chain shops; good for jewelry and clothing that actually feels distinct to Guadalajara.
  • 8:00pmDinner at Tyrano — Italian, consistently rated the neighborhood favorite with especially strong pasta. Santo Coyote or Altezza la Cocina Italiana (rooftop views) are alternatives. No reservation yet.
  • 10:00pmDrinks at El Gallo Altanero — unassuming entrance leading to a festive upstairs bar overlooking a lit courtyard, live mariachi some nights, consistently rated one of the best nightlife experiences in the city (4.4★, 1,200+ reviews praising the staff by name). Or Mecenas — an intimate, unmarked cocktail den that distills its own spirits in house, no labels on the bottles, ranked among North America's Top 50 Bars by reviewers (4.6★) — reservation recommended. De La O Cantina (speakeasy cocktails) and Romea (wine bar) are quieter alternatives; La Bodega de León and Gallo Cervecero are easier, lower-key options.
Alternative day trip: Guachimontones & Teuchitán — about an hour northwest of Guadalajara, home of the only known circular stepped pyramids in Mesoamerica. Open Tuesday to Sunday, 9am–5pm; plan 2.5–4 hours on site, about 8 hours with travel and a lunch by the lagoon. Would replace the brunch and shopping; still back for the 8pm dinner.
